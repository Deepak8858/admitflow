## Publication and registry decisions

Proposed public additions: `/about`, `/security`, `/contact`, `/product/whatsapp-follow-up`, `/product/admissions-recovery`, `/resources`, and only the three exact resource slugs in `src/lib/buyer-resources.ts`. Unknown slugs call `notFound()` and `dynamicParams = false`. Resource visible dates are 29 September 2026, with no invented author. Breadcrumb schema is generated from the same item names and paths as the visible breadcrumbs. Organisation/SoftwareApplication schema carries only documented name, URL, email, product category and description.

Do not add `/privacy` or `/terms` to the public registry yet. The drafts in `docs/drafts/` are internal and contain owner decisions. Do not publish pricing, customer logos/testimonials, ROI, certifications, registered legal suffix, office address or founder attribution without direct evidence.

## Follow-up issue found and fixed during content review

Previously, `recoverableLeads()` did not filter `humanOwned`, and `campaign.create` reset selected leads to `humanOwned = false`. A later campaign could therefore re-enrol a counsellor-owned lead after takeover stopped its pending jobs. The queue now excludes human-owned leads and the campaign action rejects them before enrollment. The existing explicit staff `lead.update` path for “Enable AI” remains available and returns an otherwise eligible lead to the queue. The new regression test exercises takeover, rejected enqueue without a partial campaign/job write, and explicit re-enable followed by successful enqueue. `tsx --test tests/domain.test.ts` passed 19/19 tests on 29 September 2026.
