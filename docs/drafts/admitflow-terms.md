## Product scope available to describe

AdmitFlow is an admissions workspace for coaching-institute staff. The current product supports lead records and fixed stages, counsellor ownership, review-led follow-up, configured WhatsApp messaging and AI modes, optional one-way Google Calendar booking sync, and records for admissions receipts and refunds. A demo workflow is not a live provider send. Availability, approved templates and provider permissions depend on the institute's setup. The product is being prepared for pilot use; no customer outcome or savings commitment has been established.

## Decisions required for an agreement

1. **Parties and acceptance:** verified legal service operator and address, institute contracting party, authorized signatory, electronic acceptance method and effective date.
2. **Scope and pilot:** what is included in a pilot; onboarding, support and migration responsibilities; what constitutes acceptance or completion; whether any service-level promise is offered.
3. **Commercial terms:** approved price, currency, taxes, billing trigger, trial length if any, renewal, cancellation, refunds and the distinction between SaaS billing and the institute's admission-payment records. Do not infer these from code defaults.
4. **Data and privacy:** institute and operator roles, DPA where needed, export/deletion at exit, full retention and backup periods, subprocessors and transfer rules.
5. **Messaging responsibilities:** who supplies valid WhatsApp consent and template approval, Meta business account and permissions, provider charges and opt-out handling. An inbound service reply is not future campaign consent.
6. **AI and human review:** permitted use of autonomous/assisted/paused modes, staff review obligations, prohibited uses and responsibility for admissions decisions. A message-count guard is not a monetary spend cap.
7. **Security and incidents:** approved security schedule, access responsibilities, incident notification and operational evidence. No certification or audit claim has been established for public terms.
8. **Availability and liability:** support route and hours, provider outages, warranties, liability limits, indemnities, dispute resolution and governing law. These require legal and commercial approval.
9. **Termination:** notice periods, data retrieval window, credential disconnection, deletion and surviving obligations, with a policy that matches the implemented data lifecycle.
